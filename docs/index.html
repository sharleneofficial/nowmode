In NowMode the watch rests on NOW and a tap moves through the sky in words: the moon, the sun, what’s overhead. Press the button or say “ClockTime” and the same taps give times: the clock, sunrise and sunset, when the next shower peaks. Press again or say “NowMode” to go back to words.

MyriadFlow produces every NowMode and makes it phygital: a chip in the bezel links the watch to its digital record. Hold your phone to it to check the watch is genuine and see that record.

  1. Tap to prove it

    Hold your phone to the contactless mark on the bezel. It confirms a genuine NowMode.

  2. See its record

    Its edition number, who made it, and how it was made. The record stays with the watch, even if it changes hands.

  3. Its own sky

    The record also holds the sky on the day the watch was made: the moon’s phase and the planets in view. No two are the same.
